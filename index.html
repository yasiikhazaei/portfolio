THE CHALLENGE

A valuable retention feature had very low awareness while people manually repeated the same searches.

THE EVIDENCE

Feedback, surveys, usability tests, analytics events, and Clarity recordings connected discovery issues to notification failures.

THE DIRECTION

Remove restrictions, surface the feature in results, prompt in context, and repair notification timing and relevance.

ROLLOUT4 stages
